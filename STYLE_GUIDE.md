# DARE Design Constitution & Tone

> **Core Principle**: DARE is a real-world, authentic community challenge and lifestyle platform. The design and voice follow an Apple / Nike-inspired aesthetic: clean, modern, premium, minimalist, and grounded in real human accomplishment.

---

## 1. Visual Style

- **Aesthetic**: Clean, modern, premium, minimalist.
- **Palette**: Dark slate backgrounds (`#090D16`, `#0F172A`, `#1E293B`), refined subtle borders (`border-slate-800`), crisp white text, and focused accent highlights (vibrant emerald, clean amber/gold, sleek pink/rose).
- **Typography**: Clean, elegant sans-serif hierarchy (system font stacks, Inter / SF Pro aesthetic). Crisp weights, balanced line heights, no bloated or chaotic layouts.
- **Components**: Polished cards, subtle glass/translucent overlays, clean rounded corners (12px–20px), precise micro-interactions.
- **Imagery**: Studio-grade product renders, real human activity photography, authentic fitness/challenge shots, clean metallic emblems with natural soft lighting and shadows.

---

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone

- **Voice**: Real-world, energetic, authentic, empowering, and motivational.
- **Tone**: Focused on community, personal growth, athletic & creative dares, proof verification, and camaraderie.
- **Terminology**: 
  - Use: **Member**, **Creator**, **Challenger**, **Community**, **Dares**, **Proof**, **Streak**, **Cred**, **Reward**.
  - Avoid: Sci-fi roleplay or video game fantasy terms.

---

## 3. STRICT PROHIBITIONS

Every contributor, agent, and engineer working on this codebase **MUST** observe these strict rules:

1. **NO Cyberpunk or Sci-Fi Aesthetics**:
   - Strictly NO neon laser grids, circuit board traces, robotic/cyborg elements, or dystopian glitch art.
   - Strictly NO holographic wireframes, matrix code rain, or tech-fantasy tropes.
2. **NO Sci-Fi or Gaming Jargon**:
   - Strictly NO words like:
     - `cyber`, `cyberpunk`, `cyberspace`, `cybernetic`
     - `neural`, `neural node`
     - `quantum`, `quantum vault`
     - `holo`, `holo pod`
     - `operative`, `stealth agent`
     - `matrix`, `relic`
3. **Product Assets**:
   - All product images, badges, and icons must look like real, tangible, premium lifestyle or fintech goods (e.g., solid brushed gold/silver coins, elegant gift boxes, polished trophy cases).
   - Zero glowing neon circuits or cyberpunk laser effects.
